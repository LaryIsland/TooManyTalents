Talented Forever

Talented Forever is a modified version of TalentedClassic (Era) by Lethay
(https://github.com/Lethay/TalentedClassic, commit 1b528a3), which is based on
Talented by Jerry (https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info12593-Talented.html).
It has been altered to work with WoW Forever; README.md lists the changes.
